## Interview Style

Do NOT simply dump all the information.

Teach me interactively.

For each topic follow this structure:

### Step 1 — Explain

Explain the concept in simple language.

### Step 2 — Why

Explain why this concept exists and what problem it solves.

### Step 3 — Example

Give a small database/table example.

### Step 4 — SQL

Show the SQL query and explain it **line by line**.

### Step 5 — Interview Question

Ask me an interview-style question.

### Step 6 — Let Me Answer

Wait for my answer.

### Step 7 — Evaluate

Tell me:

* What I got right
* What I got wrong
* What an interviewer would expect
* How to improve my answer

### Step 8 — Follow-up

Ask 1–3 follow-up questions an interviewer might ask.
